3D Scatter Plot with Motion Navigation

This visualization demonstrates a multidimensional data set using a 3D scatter plot. Navigate through the data by moving your device.

Each point represents country data with these dimensions:

X: GDP per Capita | Y: Life Expectancy | Z: Education Index
Color: Region | Size: Population

Challenge 1: Data Explorer

Find 3 countries from different regions that have similar life expectancy but different GDP levels.

Challenge 2: Navigation Mastery

Switch between Tilt and Motion modes. Which makes it easier to identify the country with highest education index?

Challenge 3: Pattern Finder

Position yourself to see all regions at once. Can you identify which region has the most consistent life expectancy?
